PURCHASE PROTECTION

Cancellation & Refunds

This is a draft policy. It must be finalised against the actual products, tournament arrangements, payment gateway and applicable Indian consumer law before payments are enabled.

1. Current demo status

No paid subscriptions, event registrations or other paid services can be purchased through this preview. No payment is due to access this demo website.

2. Future digital subscriptions

The final policy will specify whether a subscription renews automatically, how to cancel, when access ends, and whether refunds are available for unused periods, duplicate charges, technical failures or unauthorised transactions. Applicable statutory consumer rights will not be restricted.

3. Tournament and event fees

For event fees, the organiser's published cancellation, postponement and abandonment rules will be displayed before registration. The organiser and platform responsibilities will be clearly identified at checkout.

4. Refund requests

When paid services launch, requests should include the order/transaction reference, registered email and reason. Never include card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN or full banking credentials. A verified support email and response timeline must be inserted before launch.

5. Processing

Approved refunds will be routed to the original payment method where supported by the payment provider. Actual credit timelines depend on banks and payment networks and will be communicated at the time of approval.
