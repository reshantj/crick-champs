Privacy Policy

This draft must be completed and reviewed before production. The final policy must accurately describe the data actually collected, vendors, retention periods, grievance process and user rights.

1. Information we may process

Depending on features you use, the platform may process account name, email address, profile details, team membership, match and performance records, tournament registrations, support messages, device/browser logs and transaction references. Do not collect sensitive information unless necessary and lawful.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, display profiles and match information according to your privacy settings, operate tournaments, provide support, prevent fraud, process payments through payment partners and improve reliability.

3. Public and private information

Some cricket information may be displayed publicly when you or an authorised organiser chooses to publish it. Contact details, authentication data and private account information should not be publicly displayed. Review profile and team visibility settings before publishing.

4. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers required to operate the platform (such as hosting, database, email and payment providers), tournament organisers where necessary, or authorities where legally required. Vendors should be assessed and contractually restricted to their assigned purpose.

5. Security

We intend to use access controls, encryption in transit, database row-level security and security monitoring. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should use unique passwords and protect their email accounts.

6. Retention and deletion

Information should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal obligations, dispute resolution and security. The production service must provide a verified process to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

7. Children

Where minors use the platform, age-appropriate safeguards and parent/guardian consent processes must be implemented as required by applicable law before collecting their personal data.

8. Cookies and analytics

The production site may use essential storage for authentication and security. Any analytics, advertising or non-essential tracking must be disclosed and configured in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, withdraw consent or raise a grievance through the official contact channel. Insert the operator's verified privacy/grievance email before launch.

10. Updates

This policy may be updated when features, vendors or legal requirements change. The latest version and effective date should be published on this page.
