PURCHASE INFORMATION

Paid Services

No paid plan or tournament payment is enabled in this preview. Do not transfer money to anyone claiming to sell Crick Champs access until an official checkout is published.

Potential future services

  • Optional player analytics and profile enhancements.
  • Tournament organiser tools and registration management.
  • Optional media, streaming or event services where available.

Price disclosure

Before a paid service is launched, its exact INR price, GST/taxes where applicable, billing period, included features, renewal terms and cancellation rules will be shown on the checkout page. The preview's “Coming soon” labels are not offers for sale.

Payment processing

Payments, if introduced, will be processed through a named payment gateway. The platform should retain only the transaction reference, amount, currency, status and necessary reconciliation information—not card CVV, UPI PIN or banking passwords.

Receipts and support

Order confirmations and receipts should be sent to the email provided at checkout. The operator must publish verified support and grievance contacts before taking payments.
