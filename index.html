06 / ABOUT & FAQ
CCCRICK CHAMPSYOUR GAME. YOUR GLORY.

Crick Champs is being built as a digital home for grassroots and community cricket—connecting players, teams, organisers and fans through the moments that matter.

Our goal is to make local cricket easier to discover, organise, follow and celebrate.

Is Crick Champs live?

This is the first website preview. Live scoring, user accounts, tournament operations and payments will activate after backend integration and testing.

Can I create a player or team profile?

The screens are prepared for those features. Profile creation and saving will be enabled after Supabase authentication and database integration.

Will I have to pay to join?

Community access is planned to be free. Any optional paid service will show its full price and terms before checkout.

How do I report an issue?

Use the contact details published by the platform owner once official support email and phone are configured. Do not submit sensitive personal or payment information through this demo.
